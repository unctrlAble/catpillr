# Privacy Policy
###### last update july 25
## 1. What Catpillr Collects & Stores
To keep track of stats in your server, Catpillr Bot only stores:
- Your Discord User ID (to keep track of who scored what)
- Server ID & Channel IDs (to know which server and channels the stats belong to)
- Word & Message Counts (the actual numbers and tallies the bot tracks)
Message Content Policy: Catpillr Bot reads incoming messages strictly to count words and update your tallies.
No raw chat messages, text history, or conversation content is ever saved or stored.
Once the word is counted, the message text is discarded immediately.

## 2. How Your Data Is Used
The data stored is used only to display stats and word counts when you run bot commands in your server.
We will never sell, share, or trade your data with anyone else.

## 3. Data Deletion
If you don't want Catpillr Bot to hold onto your User ID or stats anymore, you can get your data erased anytime:
Just dmn cold (me) on discord and I'll manually delete your user ID and all associated stats from the database.

## 4. Questions & Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or want your data wiped, dm cold on discord.
