# Terms of service
## 1. What Catpillr Collects & Stores
- Your Discord User ID, Server ID, and Channel IDs.
- Word / Message Counts (the stats Catpillr keeps track of).

**What we DO NOT store**: Catpillr looks at messages, but we never save your actual messages or chat history.
Once the count is updated, the message text is **discarded instantly**.

## 2. How to Delete Your Data
If you don't want Catpillr holding onto your user ID or stats anymore then just reach out.

## 3. Rules & Liability
- Don't try to crash the bot or spam commands to break the database.
- Catpillr is a fun side project provided "as is."
- If Discord goes down or the bot misses a count, don't sweat it—we're not responsible for lost stats or server downtime.

Follow Discord's own Terms of Service while using the bot.

## 4. Questions?
If you have questions, or want your data deleted, contact cold (me ;3) on Discord. (I don't have a support server)
